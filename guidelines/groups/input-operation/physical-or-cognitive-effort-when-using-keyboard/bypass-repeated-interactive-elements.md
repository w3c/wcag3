A :term[mechanism] is available to move :term[keyboard focus] past blocks of :term[interactive elements].

:::applies-when
A block of three or more interactive elements is repeated on a :term[page]/:term[view] within a :term[set of pages/views].
:::

:::except-when
The block of content is the last content block on a page/view, and a mechanism is available to move keyboard focus to the top of the page.
:::
