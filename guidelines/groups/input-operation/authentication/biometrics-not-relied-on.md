:term[Biometrics] are not relied on as the only way to achieve any functionality except when a biometric is essential to the functionality.

:::note
Biometrics includes measurements related to fingerprints, voice, face, body shape, gait, DNA, and the measurement of anything else to do with our biology or physical movements.
:::

:::note
Some uses of biometrics includes verification of identity, speaker identification, dress or shirt size determination, emotion recognition, and diagnosis to name a few.
:::

:::note
There are multiple ways to establish verification of identity that do not rely on biometrics so biometrics are useful but not essential for identity verification.
:::

:::note
High security applications may be exempt from this accessibility requirement but that would be policy decision - and the application would remain non-conformant to WCAG.
:::
