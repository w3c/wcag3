title: No flashing over threshold (no exceptions)
issueLabel: No flashing (no exceptions)
---

:term[Flashes] are below the :term[general flash and red flash thresholds] without a minimum size.

:::applies-when
- Content includes :term[flashes].
:::

:::note
There are people who may not know they are susceptible to photosensitive seizures, and people who may have a much larger view of content than the default (zoomed in or with the display close to the eye).
:::
