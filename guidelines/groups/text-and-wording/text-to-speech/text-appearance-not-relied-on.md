Meaning conveyed by :term[text] style properties is also :term[programmatically determinable].

:::applies-when
* the styling, weight, or other text characteristics convey meaning beyond what the text itself says.
:::

:::except-when
* emphasis is the only additional meaning conveyed.
:::

:::example
* When bold or italics is used to indicate an error, an additional indicator such as an exclamation mark (!) is also provided.
* When larger text is used as a hint in a puzzle, an additional indicator such as an asterisk (*) is also provided.
:::
