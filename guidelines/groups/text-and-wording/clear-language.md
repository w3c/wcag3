Users can understand the content without having to process complex or unclear language.


:::ednote
This :term[guideline] will include exceptions for poetic, scriptural, artistic, and other content whose main goal is expressive rather than :term[informative].
:::

:::ednote
See also: [Structure](#structure) as these guidelines are closely related.
:::

:::ednote
To ensure this guideline works well across different languages, we are working with W3C's Global Inclusion community group, the Internationalization (i18n) task force, and others to review and refine the testing and techniques for these provisions. If you’d like to partner with us, please contact kevin@w3.org.
:::
