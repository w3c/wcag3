A :term[mechanism] is available that allows users to show or hide text annotations.

:::applies-when
* the text includes annotative notation to support pronunciation or reading.
:::

:::example
* Furigana (<span lang="ja">振り仮名</span>) in Japanese.
* Pinyin in Standard Chinese.
* Bopomofo or Zhuyin Fuhao (<span lang="zh-Hant">注音符號</span>) used in Taiwan.
:::
