title: Sign language available (prerecorded)
---

:term[Sign language interpretation] is provided for all prerecorded :term[audio] content in the primary sign language that is most appropriate for each intended audience or region.

:::except-when
- The audio content is an alternative for visual content and clearly labelled as such.
- The audio content is decorative background sound.
- The audio is an :term[interface sound effect].
:::

:::example
Audio description is an example of audio content that is an alternative for visual content.
:::
