Background sounds in prerecorded media content are lower than the foreground speech content by at least 20 decibels, or a mechanism is available to:
* Turn off background sounds, or
* Adjust background sounds to be lower than the foreground speech by at least 20 decibels

:::applies-when
Prerecorded media content includes foreground speech and background sounds.
:::

:::except-when
* The foreground speech does not convey meaning.
* Background sounds last for one second or less, for every ten seconds of speech.
* The difference between foreground and background audio is essential to conveying the meaning of the content.
* The content is an audio CAPTCHA or audio logo. 
* The combined foreground and background audio is musical expression.
:::

:::note
Not all speech is considered foreground speech. For example, background conversations not relevant to the prerecorded event.
:::

:::note
Not all parts of the prerecorded media content may have foreground speech. For example, a conversation happening in the background of a busy environment during a break in foreground speech is considered background if the audience isn't supposed to focus on it.
:::
