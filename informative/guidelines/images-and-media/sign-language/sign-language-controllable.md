## Tests

### Sign language control mechanism

<b>Procedure</b>

For each audio and video player where sign language is not hard-coded:
1. A mechanism is provided to switch on sign language interpretation.
2. A mechanism is provided to switch off sign language interpretation.
3. A link to a sign language interpreted version of the content is provided.

<b>Expected results</b>
* Either #1 and #2 are true or #3 is true.
