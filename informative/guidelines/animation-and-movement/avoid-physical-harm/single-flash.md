## Methods
* Design content without large changes of contrast. For example, when a menu with a light background is opened, it opens over a light background. Videos do not go from a dark to light background in less than a second.

## Procedure

For each change of contrast:
1. Check if the change of contrast is triggered by a loading a new :term[page]/:term[view].
2. Check that the change of contrast is less than 4.5:1.
3. Check that the area of the change of contrast is less than 30% of the viewport.

### Expected results
#1, #2, or #3 is true.
