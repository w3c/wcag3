## Tests

### Headings set at right level

<b>Procedure</b>

For the hierarchy of headings:
1. Check that headings for sibling content blocks have the same heading level.
2. Check that headings for immediate child content blocks are at most one level higher than parent content blocks.

<b>Expected results</b>
* #1 and #2 are true.


### HTML heading levels not skipped

<b>Procedure</b>

For each page/view:
1. Review the hierarchy of headings.
2. Check that each heading level is at most one numerical level higher than the preceding heading.

<b>Expected results</b>
* #2 is true.
